# generic-titles-v1: titles that do not identify a work on their own.
# Owner: entity-resolution-engineer. Referenced by policy er-v1 revision er-v1-r2
# (genericTitlesResource in er-v1.json). Changing this list changes policy behaviour: bump the
# file version (generic-titles-v2.txt), point the policy at it, and record the change in
# modules/entity-resolution/POLICY-CHANGELOG.md.
#
# Format: one entry per line, already in normalized form (TextNormalize.normalizeTitle: markup
# removed, trailing period dropped, folded to lower-case ASCII-ish tokens separated by one
# space). Lines starting with '#' and blank lines are ignored.
#
# Matching (scholarchain.er.policy.GenericTitles.isGeneric): a title is generic when its
# normalized form, or its normalized form with purely numeric and roman-numeral tokens removed
# ("Editorial 2018", "Erratum II"), equals an entry. An empty normalized title is also treated
# as generic.
#
# Effect (MatchPolicy, er-v1-r2): a work pair where either title is generic is never ACCEPTED
# on title, year, author or venue equality (exact-bibliographic or score path, or LLM). Only
# an exact-identifier agreement can accept it; otherwise it is UNRESOLVED, or REJECTED when
# the score is below the reject threshold. Generic titles are also excluded from the
# researcher title-similarity feature and from same-work bibliographic evidence.
